# Terms of Use — Kanal Otomasyonu

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Kanal Otomasyonu ("the tool") is a private, internal tool. Its only user is its owner, an individual YouTube creator who uses it to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels and to read analytics for those channels. The tool is not offered, distributed or sold to anyone else.

## YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Use of YouTube data through the tool is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and to the tool's own [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Acceptable use

- The tool is used only with channels owned by its user.
- Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded.
- The tool does not upload content on behalf of anyone else, does not interact with other users' content, and does not post comments on other channels.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions by removing "Kanal Otomasyonu".
